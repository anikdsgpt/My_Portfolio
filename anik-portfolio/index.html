Testing Philosophy

The principles that shape how I approach modern SDET and AI-assisted quality engineering.

Automation Must Be a Product Capability

Test automation should be designed like an internal product: reusable, scalable, and valuable across releases instead of a one-off script collection.

Coverage Should Follow Risk, Not Habit

High-value coverage comes from targeting failure-prone flows, integrations, and security-sensitive paths rather than automating low-signal checks blindly.

Security Is Part of Test Engineering

SAST, SCA, and OWASP-driven thinking should live inside quality engineering, not as an afterthought outside automation programs.

AI Should Improve Throughput, Not Create Noise

LLM and NLP-based utilities are most valuable when they reduce analysis effort, improve documentation parsing, and optimize test selection without weakening engineering rigor.

Leadership Means Better Quality Decisions

Mentoring, triage ownership, and risk-based planning matter because strong QA leadership raises the quality of team decisions, not just test execution volume.
